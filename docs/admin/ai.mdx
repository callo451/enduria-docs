# AI settings

Control which assistance your team can use and review proposed changes before they run.

> **Availability:** The central agent, revised classification controls, usage controls and shared module access rules described here are awaiting application rollout. Until rollout is confirmed, your screen may show older labels. Broader module automation remains planned separately.

## Before you start

Open **Settings → Artificial Intelligence** using an active staff account with no client binding. Viewing configuration and recent activity requires `ai.config.view`; saving requires `ai.config.edit`. The settings navigation is available to administrators. Staff use of the Agent requires `ai.copilot.use`, an allowed role, and the relevant permissions for the records or actions requested.

Your operator must configure an AI provider for this deployment. The **Models** tab shows the configured provider and model tiers. Tenant settings choose tiers; they do not supply provider credentials.

## Enable the shared staff Agent

1. Open the **Features** tab and select **Enable Agent**.
2. Choose a suitable context window.
3. Select **Save Configuration**.
4. Ask an eligible staff member to open **Agent** from the sidebar or bottom button and submit a question.

The panel, expanded workspace, briefing questions and record shortcuts share one conversation. Staff do not choose separate module assistants. Their ordinary record permissions still apply, and proposed ticket actions need a separate human decision. The existing Copilot permission key remains the access requirement.

Use the **Security** tab's usage profile to choose a starting limit. See [Using AI assistance](../product/ai-assistance) for saved drafts, context, stopping and checking action outcomes. Portal assistance has its own portal controls and requester limits.

## Enable ticket suggestions

1. Configure active priorities for the work-item types your team uses and active, unrestricted service-catalogue categories. Automatic suggestions currently omit categories restricted to selected customers or staff.
2. Open the **Features** tab and enable **Enable Smart Classification**.
3. Enable **Suggest Classification on New Tickets**.
4. Select **Save Configuration**.
5. Publish a **Built-in ticket triage** workflow in [Agent access](./agent-access.mdx#set-up-built-in-ticket-triage), with the eligible internal records and clients. The publisher also needs all-ticket view and AI configuration-edit permissions. Classification needs both this workflow and the AI setting above.
6. Open a newly created eligible support ticket or incident. Its workspace shows the queue and preparation state, then **Suggested classification** if a current suggestion is available.
7. Review the category and priority. If the published workflow includes **Review ticket classification changes**, staff with `tickets.view.all` and `tickets.edit.all` can select **Review classification**, adjust the choices, and prepare an exact change for separate approval. Otherwise, use the ordinary ticket fields. See [classification review](agent-access.mdx#review-and-apply-a-classification) for the steps and recovery rules.
8. Optionally include **Review configured ticket routing** in the publication. Configure category mappings and assignment in **Settings → Queues**. After an approved classification, eligible staff can review the selected queue and assignee as a separate change. See [routing review](agent-access.mdx#review-routing-after-classification) for permissions, supported settings and recovery. An AI model cannot choose the assignee or replace existing ownership.

Suggestions do not change the ticket's type, category, priority, queue, assignee or status automatically. Confidence is an estimate for review, not permission to execute a change. Changed sources, choices or model settings during generation make the result stale. Existing tickets and historical imports are not processed. If the model cannot choose a suitable classification, fails or times out, review manually. The workflow and these updated states are awaiting rollout.

**Enable Auto-Resolution** is unavailable. Matching knowledge or offering an answer does not confirm that an issue is resolved. **Auto-Generate on Ticket Resolve** is also unavailable; use the supported manual article-drafting workflow instead.

Start with suggestions and reviewed changes. Inspect their saved outcomes before extending the workflow to more queues or customers. Use the supported workflow controls described above; automatic changes remain unavailable unless explicitly supported by your installed release.

## Review proposed actions

Read [Using AI assistance](../product/ai-assistance.mdx) for the review steps. A proposal must use current configured vocabulary and your current permissions. AI approval does not replace a separate required ticket approval: if that review is required, create the request through its ordinary workflow.

Ticket investigation requires the appropriate ticket-view permission. Creating a general ticket requires `tickets.create`; creating an incident requires `tickets.createIncident`. Knowledge retrieval requires `kb.view` and the article's category access. These permissions do not grant access to other modules. Portal assistance uses the current requester's ticket scope and customer-visible published knowledge, including client audiences.

## Control module access

Staff assistance uses the relevant module permissions in addition to AI access. Staff accounts bound to a client must use their supported portal assistance; the staff AI surfaces do not provide tenant-wide access for those accounts.

| Information requested | Required scope |
| --- | --- |
| Asset register and reported device facts | `assets.view` for unbound staff |
| Directory profiles | `users.view`; profiles exclude credentials and permission overrides |
| Projects | `projects.view.all`, managed projects through `projects.view.own`, or member projects through `projects.view.assigned` |
| Project financial facts | Accessible project plus `projects.budget.view` |
| Service catalogue | `catalog.view`, active items and the customer's current audience where applicable |
| Ticket analytics | `tickets.view.all` or `tickets.view.own`; totals retain the account's ticket scope |

The Agent can return charts and tables with a limited number of results. A shortened group list does not mean the remaining records are absent. SLA reports keep response and resolution outcomes separate; missing clocks remain unknown. Asset analyses count active register records by default. An asset's configured status and operational state are separate facts.

Communication drafts prepare text for review. They do not send emails or notifications. Classification and queue guidance apply no changes. Catalogue guidance opens the selected service's normal request form; it does not submit a service request or bypass required fields and approvals.

Existing dedicated MCP keys keep their documented ticket operations. Published service-connection keys provide allowed module reads and reviewed ticket proposals through [Agent access](agent-access.mdx); this setup is awaiting rollout. A signed-in staff reviewer approves a ticket change. Delegated sign-in and broader automation remain planned separately.

## Review an external agent's ticket proposal

> **Availability:** The revised MCP review, cancellation and stale outcomes are awaiting application rollout. Existing saved proposals retain their original approval and rejection flow.

An external MCP client can prepare a general support ticket and return a review link. Its dedicated key cannot approve or create the ticket. Review requires an active staff account with no client binding and both `tickets.create` and `tickets.view.all`. It does not require Copilot to be enabled.

1. Open the returned Enduria review link and sign in with your staff account.
2. On **Review ticket proposal**, check the title, description, priority and category. You will become the ticket requester and be recorded as its creator and approver.
3. Select **Approve and create ticket**, **Reject**, or, for a new proposal, **Cancel proposal**.
4. Read the recorded status. An **executed** result includes a link to the created ticket. Rejection and cancellation create no ticket.

New proposals expire after at most 30 minutes, or sooner if the proposing key expires. Current staff permissions, key authority, API enablement and configured ticket choices are checked again at approval. If relevant configuration changed, the proposal becomes **stale** and no ticket is created. Request a fresh proposal and review its updated fields. A required ordinary ticket approval must still use the normal ticket workflow.

If a decision response is lost, retry the same decision or select **Reload proposal** to inspect its recorded outcome before asking the client for another proposal. The client must reuse its request key for retries of the same fields. Different fields require a new request key and review. If the proposing key was revoked, approval is refused; an authorised reviewer can still inspect and reject or cancel the saved intent.

Read-only dedicated ticket keys can search bounded organisation-wide ticket summaries; they cannot prepare proposals. Those ticket tools do not provide asset actions, assignment, remote remediation or OAuth connection setup. Service keys use the separately published module and client scope in [Agent access](agent-access.mdx). An operator must enable and configure the endpoint before an external client can connect.

## Limits and retained evidence

Set a usage profile to keep AI activity within your team's limits:

1. Open the **Security** tab.
2. In **AI usage limits**, choose **Standard** or **Lower usage**. Lower usage allows shorter requests and fewer simultaneous calls.
3. Select **Save Configuration**.
4. Use **Recent AI activity → Refresh activity** to check recent requests.

These limits cover staff assistance, portal help and background AI work. A request may make several model calls, including retries. Increasing a limit affects new requests; a running request keeps its original ceiling. Reductions apply before its next model call. Separate **Request limits** control how often people can start AI requests.

Recording caption batches share one request budget. If a batch reaches a limit, no caption changes from the refused batch are saved. Use a smaller batch where supported, or ask your administrator to adjust the limits. When knowledge AI is disabled, basic captions can still be prepared. Review captions before publishing.

Open **Advanced limits and cost estimates** only when you need individual limits or a cost estimate. Tokens measure the amount of text processed. A call limit of 0 pauses new calls. Enter input and output prices per million tokens for the models shown, select **Use entered prices**, then **Save Configuration**. Prices use the displayed currency. A price of 0 must be an explicit choice; an unknown price is left unavailable.

The optional **Estimated hourly cost limit** uses your entered prices. It is an estimate, not an invoice; provider discounts and additional charges may differ. If a required price is missing, or unpriced calls remain in the last hour, new calls pause until the cost limit can be checked. Leave the amount blank to use the other usage limits without an estimated cost limit.

**Recent AI activity** shows up to 25 requests. **Response finished** means the AI response ended; it does not mean a proposed ticket change ran. Check the action's own review. **Usage unavailable** keeps a cautious allowance against the limit until confirmed usage arrives. **Cost unavailable** means no compatible price is recorded. Neither means zero usage or free service. The **Support reference** can help your operator investigate a request without sharing conversation text.

Use the remaining **Security** controls for log retention. Mandatory action decisions, model-call evidence and scheduled delivery claims are kept separately from ordinary expiring activity logs. This release does not provide automatic content purging for those retained records.

## Common problems

| Problem | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Configuration cannot load | Select **Retry**. Ask an administrator to check your configuration permissions if it persists. |
| No classification suggestion appears | Check feature settings, active categories and applicable priorities, provider availability, and request limits. A changed ticket may require a new manual investigation. |
| Proposal is unavailable or expired | Start a new proposal with current information. Do not reuse a decision from another account. |
| A separate ticket approval is required | Use ordinary ticket creation and its configured review. |
| The assistant fails to respond | Retry the response or contact your operator. Check the action's current status before asking for another change. |
| A usage limit pauses AI | Check recent activity and wait for the hourly allowance to recover, or adjust your limits. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same action. |
| A cost limit pauses AI | Check prices for every configured model and whether recent requests have unavailable prices. Wait for those calls to leave the hourly window, or clear the estimated cost limit while keeping other usage limits. |
