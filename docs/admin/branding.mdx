# Branding

Enduria is white-label-first. Your primary colour, logo, portal content, and related settings shape the experience without changing the platform's fixed success, warning, danger, and information semantics.

Choose a primary colour with sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes. Check navigation, buttons, focus rings, links, status information, and the customer portal after a change.

Portal layout blocks can be shown or hidden in branding settings. Custom domains must resolve to the correct organisation before users can reach the branded portal.
