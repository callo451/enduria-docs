# Outbound email

Outbound settings decide how Enduria sends mail for your organisation. Open **Settings → Email Integration**. You need the **Manage Settings** (`settings.manage`) permission.

## Choose a provider

### Platform email

1. Select **Platform email**. The description shows the address mail is sent from.
2. Enter a **Sender name** — the name recipients see, usually your company or service desk name.
3. Enter a **Reply-To address** so replies reach you. Use a mailbox connected under **Inbound Email** if replies should become ticket comments.
4. Select **Save Configuration**.

The page shows **Recipients see:** with the exact sender recipients will see, for example `"Acme Support" <noreply@…>`. The address part is the operator's and cannot be changed.

If **Platform email** is unavailable, the operator of your installation has not configured it; use custom SMTP.

### Custom SMTP

1. Select **Custom SMTP Server**.
2. Enter the **SMTP Host** and **SMTP Port** your provider gives you. Port 587 normally uses STARTTLS; port 465 uses SSL/TLS. The server must have a public address. A mail server on a private network — such as `10.0.0.5` or a name that resolves to one — is refused unless the operator of your installation allows it.
3. Turn **Use SSL/TLS** on for port 465 and off for STARTTLS ports. Enduria requires an encrypted connection by default.
4. Enter the **SMTP Username** and **SMTP Password**. Some providers require an app password rather than your normal password.
5. Enter the **Sender name** and a **From address** your SMTP account is allowed to send as.
6. Optionally enter a **Reply-To address**.
7. Select **Save Configuration**.

The password is stored encrypted and shown masked afterwards. Leave the masked value unchanged to keep the stored password; type a new one to replace it.

## Test the connection

Select **Test Connection**. Enduria checks the configured provider without sending a message: for SMTP it connects and signs in; for platform email it checks the platform sender. Fix any reported problem before relying on email; see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting).

The page header shows where your settings stand:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not configured** | No provider is set up, or the SMTP settings are incomplete. |
| **Saved, not verified** | The settings are saved, but no connection test or test email has succeeded with them yet. Changing the provider, From address or any SMTP connection setting returns to this state, and so does a failed test. |
| **Verified** | The last connection test or test email with the current settings succeeded. |

Saving settings does not verify them; only a successful test does.

## Sending limits

Enduria limits how many emails your organisation sends per hour and per day.

- With **platform email**, the operator sets the maximum. The page shows it beside the rate-limit field. You can choose a lower hourly limit, not a higher one.
- With **custom SMTP**, you choose the limit.

Limits count calendar hours and days in UTC. When a limit is reached, further email is not sent and appears in **Settings → Email Logs** as failed with *The hourly sending limit has been reached* (or *daily*). Invoices, quotes, scheduled reports, appointment reminders and notifications about email-created tickets try again later automatically; other notifications are skipped.

[System email](./overview#system-email) — password resets and invitations — has its own allowance, so reaching your notification limit never stops people signing in. Each address can also receive only a limited number of system emails, booking confirmations and survey alerts per hour, which stops these being used to flood one inbox.

## Turn email off

**Global Notifications** under **Advanced Settings** turns outbound email for your organisation on or off. When it is off, Enduria sends no notifications, documents, reports or surveys. [System email](./overview#system-email) — password resets and invitations — still goes out.

## What uses these settings

Ticket and incident notifications, the notification digest, replies to inbound email, invoices and quotes, scheduled reports, appointment reminders, satisfaction surveys and booking confirmations all use these settings.

Password resets, invitations and welcome messages are [system email](./overview#system-email): they are sent through platform email where the operator offers it, and through these settings otherwise.

## Delivery history

**Settings → Email Logs** lists every email Enduria sends for your organisation, with its delivery status. Notification emails can be resent from their details if they failed; Enduria resends through your current provider and allows a limited number of resends. Emails that contain a password-reset link or a temporary password are logged without their content and cannot be resent.
