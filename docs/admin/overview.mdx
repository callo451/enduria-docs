# Administration overview

Organisation settings control identity, access, terminology, service configuration, branding, notifications, and integrations. Settings are tenant-scoped: changing one organisation does not change another.

Before making a broad change:

1. Confirm you are working in the intended organisation.
2. Understand which users or customers the setting affects.
3. Record current behavior if a rollback may be needed.
4. Test with a limited account or connection where practical.

Start with [users and permissions](./users-and-permissions), [branding](./branding), or [integrations](./integrations).

## Asset management

Start with [the asset register](../product/assets) to configure categories, locations and lifecycle vocabulary. Use [asset approval settings](../product/asset-approvals) to choose independent reviewers, and [the lifecycle guide](../product/asset-lifecycle) to understand archiving, restoration, disposal and holds. Check each guide's availability notes before changing your organisation's procedures.

When available, [asset event rules](../product/asset-event-rules) notify selected staff about retained asset activity and highlight reviews for people who already have authority. They do not replace reviewer policies or perform asset decisions.
