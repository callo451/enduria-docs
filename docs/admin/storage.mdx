# Storage settings

**Settings → Storage** requires the settings management permission.

## Your organisation's usage

The page shows the files your organisation has stored. Only your organisation's files are counted.

- **Ticket attachments**: files uploaded to tickets or received by email.
- **Knowledge images**: images managed by knowledge-base articles.
- **Project documents**: document entries registered against projects. Their sizes are as registered.

The figures are the sizes Enduria recorded when each file was stored. They are not a bill from your storage provider.

## Hosted service

On the hosted service, storage is managed for you and there is nothing to configure. Download links expire after a short time and are issued only after the access checks for the record they belong to.

## Self-hosted installs

On a self-hosted (community) install, the page also shows:

- the active backend
- its non-secret settings (bucket, region, endpoint, credential source, encryption)
- any configuration problems
- a **Test connection** button, which writes, reads and deletes a small probe file

The backend is set with environment variables when the application starts, so it cannot be changed from this page. See [File storage](../self-hosting/file-storage.mdx) to configure it, move to S3, or clean up stored files.

## Who can download a file

A ticket attachment can be downloaded only by someone who can read its ticket. Customers can download attachments on their own requests. Removing someone's access to a ticket also removes their access to its files.
