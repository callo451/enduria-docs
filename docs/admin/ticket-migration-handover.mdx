title: Confirm performed ticket handover
description: Retain real rehearsal, routing and intake checks for independent confirmation of a ticket source transfer.
---

> **Awaiting application rollout.** Performed handover confirmation is awaiting
> application rollout. Documentation publication does not establish availability
> in your Enduria deployment.

Use **Settings → Service Management → Ticket migration → Confirm performed
handover** to compare your actual source transfer evidence and obtain confirmation
from a different administrator. The retained record brings together the earlier
rehearsal, frozen final export, selected tickets and performed intake checks.

Confirmation records what was reviewed at that point in time. Enduria does not
change your source routing, shut down the source or reverse routing for you.
Imported backlog keeps **no SLA clocks**.

If an issue is discovered after confirmation, use **Record or review a handover
exception** to [retain the issue and independently review a response](./ticket-migration-exceptions).
Response approval does not perform a reversal or change existing ticket work.

## Required access

Recording, reading, reviewing and recovering handover commands require current
active unbound staff with `settings.manage`, `tickets.manage`, `tickets.view.all`
and `tickets.edit.all`. Portal customers and client-bound staff cannot administer
this transfer. A different authorised administrator confirms or rejects the
proposal; only its proposer can cancel it while it is pending.

## Prepare the actual transfer

1. Retain and independently review the earlier rehearsal's
   [mapping plan](./ticket-migration-plans) and complete
   [evidence reconciliation](./ticket-migration-evidence). Perform the rehearsal
   in a disposable environment and keep its execution report, SHA-256, findings
   and discrepancy dispositions.
2. Freeze and capture the final source export. Retain its evidence and reviewed
   plan. Complete [activation](./ticket-migration-activation) or
   [adoption](./ticket-migration-adoption) for all selected targets and resolve any
   [corrections](./ticket-migration-corrections).
3. Inventory the source's mailbox, portal, staff web, API, integration and other
   intake paths. Perform the agreed routing work, then test real intake in the
   intended tenant, client and channel. Record actual times with an explicit
   timezone, for example `2026-10-12T09:00:00+11:00`.
4. After **all** intake checks, record and independently approve a fresh
   [selected-ticket baseline](./ticket-migration-reconciliation). Earlier
   approval does not cover later checks or intervening ticket activity.
5. Keep manual recovery instructions for each path, including how to account for
   new requests, replies and other work received meanwhile.

The earlier and final exports must belong to the same source and client scope.
The earlier plan can have been superseded by the final plan. The comparison
checks exact source identities, revisions, hashes and final dispositions. Give
every selected rehearsal identity absent from the final export its own reason.

The environment, execution report, findings, external inventory and performed
routing times are your attestations. Enduria checks retained records and actual
native intake separately; attaching a declaration does not prove that external
work happened.

## Prepare and compare the handover

1. Open **Confirm performed handover** and expand **Prepare performed handover
   evidence**.
2. Under **Choose the final selected baseline**, select the **Registered source**,
   use **Load selected-ticket baselines**, and choose the final comparison.
   Inspect its pinned reconciliation if necessary. Its retained approval must
   still be current.
3. Under **Retain the earlier performed rehearsal**, use **Load rehearsal plans**
   and **Load rehearsal evidence reports** to select the earlier reviewed
   records. Check the plan name and identifier, especially when plans have the
   same name. Fill in the performed rehearsal details and any absent selection
   reasons.
4. Under **Declare and check the intake paths**, acknowledge your completed
   external inventory and enter its reason. Use **Add named intake path** for
   each path, up to 20 in total. Give each a name, kind, disposition reason,
   expected destination and manual recovery instructions.
5. For an included path, enter the actual routing start/completion and intake
   check times. Use **Find actual new-intake tickets** and select the actual new
   request in the declared channel. Imported or migration-linked backlog cannot
   serve as this new-intake evidence.
6. For a mailbox path, use **Load registered mailboxes** to select its registered
   account and complete all five mail checks described below. Every active
   registered mailbox needs an explicit included or excluded disposition.
7. For an excluded path, retain its reason and recovery instructions. It certifies
   no successful routing or intake test. Do not omit a path simply because you
   are excluding it.
8. Enter **Record action reason** and choose **Compare performed handover**.
   Resolve the visible blockers, inspect the paged identity comparison, then
   choose **Record handover proposal**.

The result is a pending immutable proposal. Recording does not confirm the
handover. If current work, mappings, mailbox inputs, processing evidence or
authority changes, compare again before recording.

## Check every included mailbox

An included mailbox must be active, use IMAP, have current healthy connection
evidence and have successfully polled after routing. Select actual processing
records for five distinct checks:

| Check | Expected actual outcome |
| --- | --- |
| New intake | A new email ticket in the intended tenant and client, matching the path's selected new-intake ticket. |
| Matched late reply | A comment on the intended existing ticket from a recognised participant. |
| Ambiguous thread refusal | A quarantined message refused because its ticket references are ambiguous; no ticket or comment is certified. |
| Unmatched old reference | A new email ticket retaining an unmatched old reference. |
| Unverified participant reply | An internal comment retaining its unverified sender classification. |

Use the outcome finders in the path card and enter each performed check time.
The checks must use distinct processing records under the mailbox configuration
actually used by successful polling. A reply also needs its original comment
poll attribution to match that processing attempt.

An old comment replayed during a newer poll does not establish a fresh sender
authentication check. Legacy records with unknown context cannot certify a
successful test. Perform a fresh check instead. Never weaken sender policy merely
to make handover evidence pass.

## Independently review and confirm

1. A different authorised administrator opens the retained handover under
   **Inspect and independently confirm**. Use **Load source handovers**, or enter
   its **Retained handover UUID** and choose **Inspect current handover**.
2. Check the pinned source, client, final manifest and selected baseline. Expand
   **Performed rehearsal and path declarations** to inspect the execution report,
   findings, path dispositions, routing times, individual mail check times and
   recovery instructions.
3. Use **Review identity comparison**, **Review paths**, **Review mailboxes** and
   **Review selected targets** to page through the retained evidence. Follow the
   ticket links to inspect actual current work. Reinspect if the review becomes
   stale.
4. Enter **Review action reason**. Choose **Confirm performed handover** only when
   the current prerequisites and performed evidence match. Choose **Reject
   handover proposal** when the claim should not be confirmed.

The result is one retained independent decision. Confirmation does not create
additional tickets, reconstruct historical policy or certify application rollout.
Changing a confirmed proposal requires a new record; the original remains retained.

## Check later readiness and recover

Use **Retain current handover readiness** and **Load retained handover readiness**
to retain and inspect later point-in-time reports. New ticket work, configuration
changes, processing changes or lost authority can make current readiness stale.
The original performed-handover confirmation remains retained.

After an unreadable or lost action result:

1. Keep the displayed unconfirmed command UUID and action. Editing and new writes
   are paused, including after access is restored.
2. With the original account, use **Look up handover command**. If the exact frozen
   request remains on the screen, **Retry exact handover command** reuses it.
3. If permission changes, protected details are cleared but the recovery identity
   remains visible. Restore the required current authority and confirm that
   original command before starting another action.
4. After a confirmed receipt, inspect the current handover before another action.

Only the proposer can use **Cancel pending handover** before a decision. Cancellation
does not undo routing or received work. Before any separate manual reversal,
reconcile the requests, replies and other work received since the transfer.
