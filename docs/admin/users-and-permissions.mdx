# Users and permissions

Enduria resolves access through roles and dotted permissions such as `tickets.view.all` or `assets.manage`. The navigation and available actions reflect the signed-in user's permissions.

## Access checklist

- Give users the least access needed for their work.
- Prefer a reusable role over one-off access patterns.
- Review customer scoping when a user belongs to a client.
- Require the authentication controls appropriate for your environment.
- Remove or suspend access promptly when responsibilities change.

If a user can sign in but cannot see an expected area, compare their assigned role and permissions with a working account. Do not assume a legacy role label alone grants access.
