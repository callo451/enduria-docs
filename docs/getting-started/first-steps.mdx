# First steps

After your administrator invites you, sign in using the method configured for your organisation. Depending on policy, that may be email and password, a social provider, enterprise single sign-on, a passkey, or multi-factor authentication.

## Get oriented

The main sidebar groups work by area. What you can see depends on your role and permissions.

1. Open **Search** to find tickets, assets, people, and knowledge quickly.
2. Check **Notifications** for assignments and changes that need attention.
3. Open your profile menu to confirm your theme and account preferences.
4. Choose the relevant workspace—such as **Tickets**, **Assets**, or **Projects**—to begin.
