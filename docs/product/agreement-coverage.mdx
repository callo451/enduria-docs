# Assets covered by agreements

Record which of a client's assets an agreement covers, from when, and why. Coverage records are then used in three places:

- **Per-device billing** ticks covered devices in advance.
- **The client portal** names the agreements covering each device.
- **Estate reviews** list coverage by agreement.

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

</Admonition>

Agreement coverage is available to MSP organisations.

## How coverage works

A coverage record links one agreement to one asset for a span of days:

- **From** a start date.
- **Until** an end date, or, if you leave it empty, until the agreement ends. Both dates are included.
- **Reason or reference**, required. Use the evidence for the coverage, such as "Schedule B, signed 1 July" or a ticket number.

Enduria also records who added the record and when, and who ended it, when and why.

The rules:

- **Several agreements.** An asset can be covered by several agreements at once, for example a managed services agreement and a maintenance agreement.
- **No double coverage.** One agreement can't cover the same asset twice on the same day.
- **Same client.** An agreement covers only its own client's assets. If an asset moves to another client, its coverage under the old client's agreements ends that day, and the record says so.
- **Within the term.** Coverage dates must fall inside the agreement's start and end dates.
- **In force.** Coverage counts on a day only while the agreement is in force. A draft agreement, or one awaiting review, approval or signature, or terminated, covers nothing.
- **Records are kept.** Records are never edited or deleted. To change dates, end the record and add a new one.
- **No automatic coverage.** Nothing is added by discovery, imports or rules; a person always chooses the assets.

## Add assets to an agreement

You need agreement edit permission (`agreements.edit` or `agreements.manage`) and asset view permission (`assets.view`). If your account is bound to a client, you can work only with that client's agreements.

1. Open the client and select the **Agreements** tab.
2. Open the agreement's actions menu (**⋯**) and select **Covered assets**. The dialog shows how many of the client's active assets the agreement doesn't cover today.
3. Under **Add assets**:
   - Choose one or more of the client's active assets.
   - Set **Covered from**, and **Until** if the coverage ends before the agreement does.
   - Enter the **Reason or reference**.
4. Select **Add**.

Adding is all or nothing: if any chosen asset can't be covered on those dates, nothing is added and the dialog says why.

## End coverage

1. In **Covered assets**, select **End** beside the record.
2. Enter the last covered day and why the coverage ends.
3. Select **End coverage**.

The record moves to **Ended**. You can end a record only once, and never later than an end date it already had.

An agreement that has covered assets can't be deleted. Terminate it instead, so the record of what it covered is kept.

## Where coverage shows

| Where | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Covered assets** (agreement) | Current and ended records with dates, reasons and who added or ended them. Anyone who can view agreements can open it; only editors see the add and end controls. |
| **Agreements** card (asset **Overview**) | Each agreement covering the asset, current first, with dates and the reason. Anyone who can view the asset sees it. |
| **Billing** (usage review) | Each device's coverage for the period, explained below. |
| **Client portal** | The names of the client's own agreements covering each device today, on the **Devices** list and each device's page. No dates, reasons or prices. |
| **Estate reviews** | **Covered by your agreements**: each agreement in force with how many assets it covers, and the active assets no agreement covers. See [Client estate reviews](./client-estate-reviews). |

## Per-device billing

When you review usage for a billing period (**Billing → Agreements**, choose the agreement, then **Review usage**), each device shows its coverage under that agreement for the period:

- **Covered for the whole period**: ticked in advance and listed first.
- **Covered for part of the period**: shown, but not ticked.
- **Not covered by this agreement**: shown, but not ticked.

You still review every device and confirm the full period before approving, exactly as before. Coverage never changes a prepared period, an approved quantity or an invoice, and nothing is prorated.

## Audit

Adding and ending coverage are recorded in the audit log against the agreement, with the assets, dates and reason.

## What agreement coverage doesn't do

- **Rules:** coverage isn't added by discovery, imports, categories or rules.
- **Proration:** partial coverage isn't charged or credited automatically.
- **Users and licences:** coverage applies to assets only.
- **Supplier warranties:** a manufacturer's warranty or support contract is recorded separately, on the asset's **Service** tab.
