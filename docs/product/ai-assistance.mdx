# Using AI assistance

Use AI assistance to investigate work and prepare changes that you can review. The available records and actions depend on your account, permissions, and your administrator's feature settings.

> **Availability:** The action-state labels and reviewed classification behavior described here are awaiting application rollout. The planned central agent panel and expanded workspace are not included in this increment.

## Ask for help

Staff can use **AI Copilot**, **AI Search**, or the AI briefing conversation when enabled. Describe the record or question clearly, check cited information, and review any proposed action. An answer is guidance; it does not establish that a ticket is resolved or a change has been completed.

## Review a proposed change

1. Look for **Review required** and **Review proposed action before execution** in the response.
2. Open the review link using the same account that requested the action.
3. Check the destination record and every saved field, including whether a note is internal or public.
4. Select **Approve action** to execute the saved proposal, or **Reject** to leave it unexecuted.
5. Read the resulting status and open the linked ticket to verify the result.

Approval uses your current permissions. Proposals expire after 30 minutes. If the proposal is wrong, reject it and request a new one; approving does not replace its saved fields. Required domain reviews still apply.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Working | A tool response is still being prepared. |
| Review required | A decision is needed; the proposed business action has not run. |
| Result available | A tool returned information. This alone is not an execution receipt. |
| Executed | The returned action state records execution. Verify the linked record. |
| Not executed | The action was denied, rejected or cancelled. |
| Failed | The response or operation failed. Inspect current status before requesting another change. |
| Outcome uncertain | The outcome needs verification; do not assume the effect did or did not happen. |

## Review ticket classification

A ticket workspace may show **AI suggestion awaiting review** with a category, priority and confidence. Review it and edit the ordinary ticket fields as appropriate. Suggestions do not automatically route, assign or resolve the ticket.

## Customer portal assistance

When enabled, the portal shows a **Support assistant** button; your organisation may give it another name. Open it, describe the issue, and select **Send**. It can help find information available to your account and prepare your own support-ticket proposal. Open its review link and check the details before approving.

**Close** hides the panel. **Stop** stops the streamed response; it does not undo an approved action. **Contact support** opens your tickets so you can continue with the support team. This link does not automatically send the conversation to a technician.

After a failed response, use the normal portal request flow if needed. Check any existing proposal or ticket first so you do not submit the same request twice. If the assistant is absent, ask your service provider whether it is enabled for your portal.
