# Using AI assistance

Use AI assistance to investigate work and prepare changes that you can review. The available records and actions depend on your account, permissions, and your administrator's feature settings.

> **Availability:** The central agent, revised action-state labels and shared module access rules described here are awaiting application rollout. Your current service may still show the older Copilot and AI Search screens.

## Ask for help

Staff use one **Agent** when enabled for their account. Open it from the sidebar or the **Agent** button at the bottom of a staff page. AI briefing questions and record shortcuts enter this same conversation. Existing AI Search links open the expanded agent workspace.

1. Open **Agent** and describe what you need in **Message the agent**.
2. Select **Send**, or press Enter. Use Shift+Enter for a new line.
3. Check the returned sources and any **Review required** action.
4. Select **Expand agent workspace** when you need more space. Your conversation, draft and selected context stay with you.

An answer is guidance; it does not establish that a ticket is resolved or a change has been completed. There is no need to choose a different assistant for each module.

## Keep your work when moving between pages

**Close agent panel** hides the panel while keeping the same conversation. Changing staff pages or moving between the panel and workspace does not start a new request. Drafts save automatically; wait for **Draft saved** before reloading the browser. If a draft cannot save, its text stays in the current app session so you can try again.

Use **Conversations** to choose a recent saved conversation. **New conversation** starts a separate task without changing earlier proposals. The agent shows the latest 20 requests in a conversation; start a new conversation when it asks you to continue there. Older AI Search records are not converted into new agent conversations.

Use **Stop response** to stop new generation calls and disconnect the streamed answer. A provider call already sent may still finish. Stopping does not reverse an applied action or cancel a pending review. After an interrupted or lost response, select **Check saved status** before starting another request. A retry of the same saved submission does not automatically send the model request again.

Earlier response details may be withheld when a source changes or becomes unreadable. Current retained action outcomes can still appear. Ask a fresh question for up-to-date guidance.

## Add a record to your next request

1. Open a ticket, active asset, accessible project or published knowledge article.
2. Select its **Ask agent** control.
3. Check the named record and select **Add context**.
4. Add your question and select **Send**.

Context applies to the next request. It does not change the target of an existing proposal. Select **Remove** to clear the next request's context, or **Dismiss** to ignore a suggested record or question. If there is already a draft, a suggested question is offered for you to add rather than replacing your text.

Use a complete ticket number or record ID when asking about one ticket. Use keywords when searching. Ticket details include a bounded recent conversation, so open the linked ticket for its complete history.

Your administrator may limit AI usage. If a request reaches a limit or cannot finish, check any saved action's review before trying again. A finished response is separate from an applied ticket change. An interrupted response does not undo an action that was already recorded.

Knowledge answers use published articles available to your account. Drafts and project pages are excluded. Portal assistance also excludes internal articles and internal ticket notes. If access changes while information is being retrieved, the assistant may ask you to try again instead of returning the earlier result.

## Investigate across modules

Use the Agent for questions about accessible projects, assets and ticket analytics. Use a complete project number or code, or an exact asset tag, serial number or record ID, when asking for one record. Use search keywords to find candidates first if you do not know the exact identifier. Open the cited workspace to inspect the complete record.

Project membership and management permissions determine which projects can appear. Project financial information needs a separate budget-view permission. Asset investigation returns selected register and reported device facts; it does not run repairs, move custody or restore an archived asset. Active assets are used by default.

Check the period and any limitations on an analytical response. Counts remain scoped to your accessible records. A short list of groups may be truncated even when the total includes the full matching cohort. A missing SLA outcome is unknown, and a percentage change from a previous period with no records is unavailable.

If specialist assistance prepares an email or notification, review the text yourself. A draft has not been sent. Queue choices and classification guidance do not apply field changes.

## Review a proposed change

1. Look for **Review required** and **Review proposed action** in the response. A completed action uses **View action outcome**.
2. Open the review link using the same account that requested the action.
3. Check the destination record and every saved field, including whether a note is internal or public.
4. Select **Approve action** to execute the saved proposal, or **Reject** to leave it unexecuted.
5. Read the resulting status and open the linked ticket to verify the result.

Approval uses your current permissions. Proposals expire after 30 minutes. If the proposal is wrong, reject it and request a new one; approving does not replace its saved fields. Required domain reviews still apply.

New ticket-action proposals also keep the access they were prepared with. Gaining broader access does not expand a saved action. **Cancel proposal** is available for these new proposals; older saved reviews may show only **Approve action** and **Reject**. Cancellation and rejection leave the proposed change unexecuted.

If the review reports **stale**, the source or configuration changed and no action ran. Request a fresh suggestion and review its updated details. After a lost response, retry the same decision or use **Reload proposal** to inspect the recorded outcome before requesting another change.

An assignment can move a ticket out of your own-ticket access. In that case the review confirms that the action was recorded and withholds the ticket details. A colleague with current access can verify the result.

Status and classification suggestions must use your organisation's current choices. A classification action needs a priority or category. If a choice has been retired or changed, request a fresh suggestion using the available choices.

External agents with published service access can also offer ticket review links. An eligible staff reviewer opens **Review proposed action**, checks **Before this change** and the saved fields, then selects **Approve action**, **Reject** or **Cancel proposal**. Approval records that reviewer as the person making the change; new tickets also use them as requester. The external agent cannot approve the change. See [Agent access](../admin/agent-access.mdx) for setup and recovery steps.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Working | A tool response is still being prepared. |
| Review required | A decision is needed; the proposed business action has not run. |
| Result available | A tool returned information. This alone is not an execution receipt. |
| Executed | The returned action state records execution. Verify the linked record. |
| Not executed | The action was denied, rejected or cancelled. |
| Failed | The response or operation failed. Inspect current status before requesting another change. |
| Outcome uncertain | The outcome needs verification; do not assume the effect did or did not happen. |

## Review ticket classification

A ticket workspace may show **Classification is queued**, then **Preparing a classification suggestion…**, followed by **Suggested classification** with a category and priority. No fields have changed automatically. Suggestions do not route, assign or resolve the ticket.

If your administrator has enabled classification review and you have all-ticket view and edit access, select **Review classification**. Compare the current fields, keep the suggestion or adjust the choices, then select **Prepare classification review**. Open **Review saved classification change**, check the exact fields, and select **Approve action**, **Reject**, or **Cancel proposal**. Approval records you as the person changing the ticket. Reload the ticket after an executed change. Without this review option, use the ordinary ticket fields.

A newer prepared review supersedes an older pending one. Changed tickets, retired choices or reduced access prevent the saved review from executing. After a lost response, reload the saved review and check its outcome before requesting another change. A completed decision does not apply twice. Each original suggestion can apply at most one reviewed change; later edits use the ordinary ticket workflow.

If the ticket changes, its old suggestion no longer applies. A timed-out or unavailable classification asks you to review manually; do not wait for it to do the work. An older suggestion may still show **AI suggestion awaiting review** and a confidence score. Confidence is an estimate, not a verified outcome or permission to change a ticket.

Your administrator controls eligible records and whether new tickets are classified through [built-in ticket triage](../admin/agent-access.mdx#set-up-built-in-ticket-triage). This workflow is awaiting rollout; automatic classification changes remain planned separately.

## Customer portal assistance

When enabled, the portal shows a **Support assistant** button; your organisation may give it another name. Open it, describe the issue, and select **Send**. It can help find information available to your account and prepare your own support-ticket proposal. Open its review link and check the details before approving.

The assistant can also find active services available to your customer account. Where it offers a service-request link, open the service's form, complete its required fields and submit through the normal request flow. Offering a link does not create a request.

**Close** hides the panel. **Stop** stops the streamed response; it does not undo an approved action. **Contact support** opens your tickets so you can continue with the support team. This link does not automatically send the conversation to a technician.

After a failed response, use the normal portal request flow if needed. Check any existing proposal or ticket first so you do not submit the same request twice. If the assistant is absent, ask your service provider whether it is enabled for your portal.
