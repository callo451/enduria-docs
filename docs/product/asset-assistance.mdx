# Asset assistance

Asset assistance uses your organisation's AI provider to explain what Enduria already records about your assets. It never changes anything: it reads, explains and links to its sources, and a person decides what to do.

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Asset assistance requires a future application release. Publishing this guidance does not deploy these features.

</Admonition>

## Where it appears

| Where | Button | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Assets → Data quality**, on a possible match, possible duplicate or fact conflict | **Explain** | What the evidence shows, how strong it is, and what each available action would do, including why a merge would be refused. See [Asset data quality](./asset-data-quality). |
| The draft **estate review** dialog | **Suggest a note** | A short covering note for the client, drafted from the review. See [Client estate reviews](./client-estate-reviews). |
| An asset's **Overview** | **Summarise** | A few sentences on the asset's state, who holds it, how it reports, its coverage, any repair, its lifecycle and agreements. |

## How answers are built

When you ask, Enduria gathers numbered facts from records you're allowed to see and sends only those to the AI provider. The answer comes back as a few sentences, and each one must name the facts it rests on.

- **Every sentence is linked.** Beside each sentence are links to its sources: the assets by their tags, or the sections of the review.
- **Ungrounded sentences are removed.** Enduria removes any sentence that cites nothing, cites a fact it wasn't given, or contains a value it wasn't sent, and tells you how many it left out.
- **You can check every fact.** Open **Facts given** to see exactly what was sent.
- **Answers are labelled.** Each one is marked as generated, with the model and time.

Answers are a reading aid. Check the cited facts before acting.

## What is sent to the AI provider

Only comparisons and descriptions are sent:

- comparisons such as "serial number: the same for asset A and asset B (compared after normalising)" or "hostname: different";
- categories, states, your status names, manufacturer and model;
- supplier and agreement names;
- dates, durations and counts;
- custody as a kind (held by a person at the client, by your staff, in a stockroom, with a supplier), never a name.

Never sent:

- serial numbers, hardware IDs, MAC and IP addresses, hostnames;
- asset names and tags (assets are called "asset A" and "asset B");
- people's names and email addresses;
- notes, asset field values, ticket titles;
- prices and costs.

The links you see show the real tags; they are part of the page, not of what was sent.

## What is kept

Nothing generated is kept. An answer is shown once and disappears when you close it or leave the page; **Ask again** generates a new one.

The AI audit log records metadata only:

- who asked, and about which record;
- how many facts were sent, and how many sentences were shown and left out;
- the model, token counts, duration and outcome.

It never records the facts or the answer.

Suggesting a note doesn't change the review. **Use this text** fills the note field, and the note is saved only when you select **Save review**.

## Turn it on

Asset assistance is **off** until an administrator turns it on.

1. Go to **Settings → AI**.
2. Under **Asset assistance**, turn on **Enable asset assistance**.
3. Select **Save Configuration**.

To use it, a person also needs:

- **Use Copilot** permission (`ai.copilot.use`);
- for **Explain** and **Summarise**: asset view permission (`assets.view`);
- for **Suggest a note**: asset manage permission (`assets.manage`).

Staff bound to a client see assistance only for that client's records. It is never available in the client portal.

Requests count towards your organisation's AI request limits in **Settings → AI → Security**.

Asset assistance uses the model your installation configures for the copilot. A self-hosted installation chooses its AI provider in its server configuration, and can use its own model.

## What asset assistance doesn't do

- **Actions:** it never merges, links, changes licences, runs remote commands or creates tickets.
- **Chat:** there are no follow-up questions; ask again for a new answer.
- **Automatic summaries:** nothing is generated on a schedule or when a page opens.
- **Client portal:** clients never see generated answers, except a note you choose to use and publish in an estate review.
