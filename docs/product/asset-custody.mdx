# Track asset custody

Custody records who or what physically holds an asset: a staff member, a customer contact, a storage location, nobody, or nobody because it is lost. Every change is kept in the asset's custody history.

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Custody tracking requires a future application release. This guide describes the intended behaviour for that release; publishing documentation does not enable it in your workspace.

</Admonition>

Custody is separate from other asset facts:

- **Ownership.** The asset's client stays the same when custody changes.
- **Lifecycle status.** Checking an asset in or out does not change its status.
- **The active register.** Checking in never archives, retires or deactivates an asset. Use [archive, restore and dispose](./asset-lifecycle) for those decisions.

## Before you start

You need asset-view permission to see custody and asset-edit permission to change it. Staff assigned to a client can only work with that client's assets.

A custodian must be compatible with the asset's client:

- **Staff members** must be active and either unrestricted or assigned to the same client.
- **Customer contacts** must be active portal users of the asset's own client. An asset without a client cannot be held by a customer contact.
- **Stockrooms** are active locations marked **Holds stock**, either organisation-wide or belonging to the asset's own client. See [Locations](./assets#locations).

The custodian list shows only compatible people, at most 50 at a time. Search to narrow it.

## Check an asset out

1. Open the asset and select the **Custody** tab.
2. Choose **Check out**. If someone already holds the asset, or it is in storage, the button is **Transfer**.
3. Choose **Person** or **Storage**, then select the custodian.
4. For a loan to a person, optionally set **Due back**. Leave it empty for an ongoing assignment.
5. Optionally record the condition and notes, then confirm.

The **Custody** tab shows the holder, when custody started, any due date, and the last recorded condition. A loan past its due date is marked **Overdue**.

## Check an asset in

1. On the **Custody** tab, choose **Check in**.
2. Select the stockroom the asset is returned to. The stockroom also becomes the asset's location. If you don't select one, the asset becomes unassigned and keeps its last recorded location.
3. Optionally record its condition, such as **Damaged** or **Needs repair**, and notes.

Only an asset held by a person can be checked in. While an asset is in storage, its location is its stockroom: to move it, check it in or out again rather than editing the location.

An asset of a tracked catalogue model that belongs to your organisation joins [inventory stock](./inventory#work-with-a-unit) when it is checked in to an organisation stockroom, and leaves stock when it is checked out. Use the asset's **Stock** card to choose a triage outcome, or when your organisation requires approval for issues from stock.

## Record a loss, recovery or condition

- **Mark lost** records that the asset is missing. Enter a reason of at least 10 characters. The asset stays in the register; archive or dispose of it separately if that is the right outcome.
- **Record as found** returns a lost asset to a person or storage location.
- **Record condition** adds a condition note without changing who holds the asset.

## Review custody history

The **Custody history** section lists every change, newest first, with who made it, when, and the before and after custodian. Changes made through an integration or an import are labelled with their source.

Changes are recorded wherever they are made: the **Custody** tab, editing the asset, **Reassign**, CSV import, creating an asset with an assignee, and the public API. History cannot be edited or deleted.

When this feature is first enabled, each asset that already has an assignee gets one entry: **Recorded when custody tracking began**. That entry shows who held the asset at that point. It does not claim when, or by whom, the asset was originally handed over.

Archived assets keep their custody history, which stays readable. Restore an archived asset before changing its custody.

## Resolve common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Custody changed since this page loaded** | Someone else changed custody first. Select **Refresh custody**, review the current holder, then try again. Your change was not applied. |
| A save fails because the connection dropped | Select the same action again without changing it. If the first attempt was saved, you see **Already recorded** and nothing is repeated. |
| The person you need is not listed | Check that their account is active and compatible with the asset's client. Customer contacts must belong to the asset's own client. |
| A client change is refused | A customer contact of the current client holds the asset. Transfer or check it in first, or change the client and a compatible custodian together. |
| Custody buttons are missing | You may have view-only access, or the asset is archived. |
| A stockroom is not listed | Only active locations marked **Holds stock** that are organisation-wide or belong to the asset's client appear. |
| You can't change a stored asset's location | Check the asset in to another stockroom, or check it out to a person. |

## Current limits

This release records custody directly. It does not yet provide:

- approval of custody changes or recipient acceptance;
- due-date reminders or an offboarding recovery list;
- inventory stock updates when an asset is checked in.

The earlier **Require approval for asset assignment** and **Track assignment history** settings were never enforced and have been retired. Custody history is always recorded.
