# Archive, restore and dispose of assets

Use **Archive** to remove an asset from the active register while keeping its record. Use the disposal workflow to record a reviewed external disposal and its supporting evidence. Restore an eligible archived record only after an independent review.

<Admonition type="info" title="Availability">

Configurable disposal reviewers and the **Asset reviews** inbox are awaiting release. Until those features are enabled in your workspace, open requests through **Archive** or **Disposals**. This guide identifies where the newer reviewer settings apply.

</Admonition>

## Before you start

These screens are for staff, not customer portal accounts. You need `assets.view` and access to the asset's current customer scope. The following permissions are additional:

| Task | Required permission |
| --- | --- |
| Archive an asset | `assets.delete` |
| Request restoration or disposal; record disposal evidence | `assets.edit` |
| Approve or reject restoration | `assets.edit` and `assets.manage` |
| Approve or reject disposal | `assets.manage` |
| Verify disposal and archive | `assets.manage` and `assets.delete` |
| Place or release a hold | `assets.manage` |

Where [reviewer policies](./asset-approvals.mdx) apply, the reviewer must also qualify under the policy retained with that request. Configured disposal verification reviewers need `assets.delete` even when rejecting submitted evidence. Selecting a reviewer does not grant permissions, and an administrator cannot approve their own request.

Your organisation's asset lifecycle labels remain unchanged by archive, restoration and verified disposal. A label alone does not establish that a device was returned, sanitised or destroyed.

## Archive an asset

1. Open the asset from **Assets**.
2. Open its actions menu and select **Archive asset**.
3. Read **Archive asset?**, then select **Archive**.

The asset leaves the active register and remains available under **Assets → Archive**. Existing agent credentials are revoked, and commands that have not been dispatched are stopped. Work already delivered to a device may still execute.

Archiving keeps the recorded customer, custody, location, lifecycle value, costs, inventory membership and related evidence. It does not return stock, release an assignment or verify disposal. Ordinary archiving uses a confirmation; it does not create an independent approval request.

## Request restoration

1. Open **Assets → Archive** and select **Review** beside the asset.
2. Check its identity, recorded lifecycle and any message explaining why restoration is blocked. Use **Disposal evidence and holds** to inspect related records.
3. Select **Request restoration**.
4. Enter the **Restoration reason** and confirm that you reviewed the asset identity and current obligations.
5. Select **Request restoration** to submit.

The request shows **Awaiting independent review**. The request retains the asset facts and applicable reviewer policy from submission. The requester can select **Cancel request** while it is pending.

### Review and restore

A different eligible reviewer opens the archived asset, reads the request and retained instructions, then:

1. Selects **Approve and restore**.
2. Enters a **Decision reason** and **Independent review reference**.
3. Confirms the asset identity and current obligations, then selects **Approve and restore**.

The existing record returns to the active register. Select **Open active asset** to continue. Its recorded custody, stock, costs and lifecycle value are preserved; restoration does not establish operational readiness.

To decline, select **Reject request** and supply a decision reason. The submitted request and decision remain recorded.

<Admonition type="important" title="Reconnect the agent with a new token">

Restoration does not reactivate old agent credentials. Issue a fresh enrollment token after restoration. Tokens bound to this asset before its archive/restoration cycle cannot reconnect it, even if an older token still appears pending.

</Admonition>

### When restoration is blocked

Restoration cannot proceed while there is an active disposal hold, an open disposal request, completed disposal, or retained disposal execution evidence. Evidence on a rejected disposal still blocks restoration. There is no override for these conditions or a supported process to erase that evidence and restore the asset.

Approval also checks that the submitted asset facts are still current. If the request must use a different reviewer policy, the requester can cancel a pending restoration and submit a new request. Existing requests do not adopt later policy changes automatically.

## Dispose of an asset

Open **Disposal and holds** from an asset, or **Disposals** in the asset-management navigation. This workflow records externally performed work; Enduria does not erase media or destroy devices.

### 1. Prepare and submit

The asset must be active, unassigned, outside inventory membership, and free of active disposal holds or another open disposal request. Resolve those conditions before submitting. Also review custody, loans, retention, contractual and other external obligations; Enduria does not automatically reconcile all of them.

1. Select **Request disposal**.
2. Enter **Reason**, **Planned disposition**, **Approved organisational procedure reference**, and **Planned sanitisation / disposition method**.
3. Confirm that you reviewed the listed obligations.
4. Select **Request disposal**.

The request enters **Awaiting approval**. Its submitted identity, custody and intended procedure remain available for review.

### 2. Obtain independent approval

A reviewer other than the requester selects **Approve request**, supplies a **Decision reason**, confirms the obligations and approves. Where disposal reviewer settings are enabled, this person must qualify for the retained approval policy.

An approved request permits the next recording step. It does not execute the disposal or certify that an external method is suitable.

### 3. Record execution evidence

After the approved work has actually occurred, a staff member with edit permission selects **Record execution evidence** and enters:

- **External operator / organisation**
- **Execution date and time**
- **Method performed**
- **Certificate / retained evidence reference**
- **Declared certificate SHA-256**
- **Execution notes**

The execution time must be after approval and cannot be in the future. Submit the evidence to move the request to **Awaiting verification**.

Keep the original certificate in your controlled evidence repository. Enduria retains the reference and declared hash; it does not fetch the certificate, keep its original file or verify its contents against that hash.

### 4. Verify and archive

A verifier must be independent of both the requester and the person who recorded the execution evidence. The approver may also verify if they meet those conditions and the applicable verification policy.

1. Review the submitted request and external evidence.
2. Select **Verify and archive**.
3. Enter the **Independent verification reference** and **Verification findings**.
4. Confirm the obligations, then select **Verify and archive**.

Successful verification records **Verified disposal** and archives the asset. Credentials are revoked and undispatched commands are stopped. The completed request remains available under **Disposals**.

### Cancel or reject a disposal request

The requester can use **Cancel request** before execution evidence is submitted. Cancellation is unavailable after evidence is recorded.

An independent eligible reviewer can use **Reject request** and retain a decision reason. Before execution evidence, the approval reviewer policy applies; afterward, the verification policy applies. Rejection keeps earlier approval and evidence. It does not undo external disposal or make the asset eligible for restoration.

## Place or release a hold

1. Open the asset's **Disposal and holds** screen, or follow **Disposal evidence and holds** from the archive.
2. Select **Place hold**, enter a **Reason**, and save.
3. To release it, a different asset manager selects **Release hold**, records a reason, and saves.

Holds can be placed on active or archived assets, including while restoration is pending. A hold blocks disposal submission, approval, execution recording and verification, as well as restoration. It does not prevent ordinary archiving and is not a legal retention hold across all Enduria modules. The person who placed it cannot release it.

## Resolve common problems

| What you see | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A review action is missing | Confirm your current asset permissions, customer scope, independence and membership in the request's retained reviewer policy. |
| No independent reviewer is eligible | For a new request, ask a settings manager to correct future routing. For an existing request, changing settings or adding a new role member does not replace its assigned people. |
| Verification is blocked after a reviewer becomes unavailable | Actual execution evidence can still be retained, but completion waits for an originally assigned, independent reviewer to regain valid access. If the sole verifier recorded the evidence, they cannot verify it; there is no reassignment override. |
| Asset facts or the request changed during review | Reopen the current record and reassess. Do not repeat a decision against outdated facts. Before disposal evidence is recorded, the requester can cancel and submit a fresh request if appropriate. |
| Earlier records disappear after changing the asset's customer | Retained requests and holds are shown only for their original customer binding. An older open request or hold may still block action. Resolve open work before changing customer ownership; there is no history-rebinding tool. |
| An older request is not listed | Asset detail shows the latest 20 requests; disposal detail also shows up to 50 holds and 100 activity entries. Earlier evidence remains retained, but complete historical browsing and evidence export are not available here. |

Completed evidence and submitted facts cannot be rewritten through these screens. These workflows do not provide permanent asset purge, disposal-evidence correction or a way to bypass independent review.
