# Manage assets

Use **Assets** to keep a register of devices, their assigned people and clients, locations, purchase details, and related service work. You can add records manually, import a CSV, or use a supported device-management connector.

## Before you start

Your account needs asset-view permission to browse the register. Creating, editing, importing, archiving and reviewing require additional permissions. In an MSP organisation, staff assigned to a client remain limited to that client even when their role includes broad asset permissions. Customer portal access is separate from the staff workspace.

An administrator's title does not bypass these limits. See [users and permissions](../admin/users-and-permissions).

## Configure your register

Before adding a large number of assets, agree your organisation's categories, locations, statuses and asset-tag format.

- Open **Settings → Asset statuses** to name the [statuses under each state](./asset-statuses) and choose each state's default.
- Open **Settings → Asset settings** to configure tag formatting.
- Use **Categories** and **Locations** in the asset-management navigation for the corresponding reference records.
- Use your own configured statuses. Examples from another organisation may not be valid in yours.
- Keep parent categories and locations within your organisation. A hierarchy cannot contain cycles; resolve active child records before retiring a parent.

## Locations

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Shared locations, client sites and stockrooms require a future application release. Publishing documentation does not change your workspace.

</Admonition>

Assets and inventory use **one list of locations**, managed in **Settings → Asset locations**. A location can also be:

- **A client site.** Choose a client to make the location available only to that client's assets. Locations without a client are available to the whole organisation. Staff assigned to a client see organisation-wide locations and their own client's sites only.
- **A stockroom.** Select **Holds stock** for places that keep inventory stock or assets checked in to storage. Inventory items can only be kept in organisation-wide stockrooms; a client's stockroom can hold that client's stored assets.

When the asset and inventory location lists were combined, every inventory location was added to the shared list unchanged. If its code was already in use, the code gained a `-STOCK-` suffix. Locations that may duplicate another are marked **Review** with an explanation. Check them, then edit or retire the duplicate.

Locations are retired, not deleted, so history that names them stays readable. You can't retire a location, stop it holding stock, or restrict it to one client while it still holds stock or assets that don't fit.

Tag previews are examples, not reservations. The saved asset receives its allocated tag. Do not depend on consecutive numbers without gaps.

## Models

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

The model catalogue requires a future application release. Publishing documentation does not change your workspace.

</Admonition>

A **model** describes one product once, for example *Dell Latitude 5440*, so assets and inventory items can refer to the same thing. Manage models in **Settings → Models**. Each model has a manufacturer, a model name, an optional part number and asset category, and a **tracking** type:

- **Tracked asset**: each unit is its own asset record, such as a laptop or phone. In inventory, a tracked model's stock is its units, each received and issued by serial number (see [Manage inventory stock](./inventory#two-kinds-of-stock)).
- **Component**: a part that can be held as an asset record when it is worth tracking, such as a drive.
- **Consumable**: counted by quantity and used up, such as cables or toner. A consumable is never an asset record, so assets can't use consumable models.

Manufacturer and model names are matched ignoring case and extra spaces, so you can't create *Dell Latitude 5440* twice under different spellings.

When you add a model and choose its asset category, its tracking starts from the category's **Track new models by serial number** setting in **Settings → Asset categories**. You can still choose any tracking type. A model's tracking can't change while stock of it is held.

### Link a model to assets and stock

Choose a **Catalogue model** when you create or edit an asset or an inventory item. The record keeps the manufacturer and model text it was recorded or discovered with; choosing a model only fills those fields when they are blank. Units received into stock take the stock item's model.

### Link existing records

Existing assets and stock items are never linked automatically. **Settings → Models → Unlinked records** groups active assets and stock items that have no model by the manufacturer and model they were recorded with, ignoring case and spacing, and shows the other spellings found. For each group:

- **Create model** opens a new model filled in from the group. Saving it also links the group.
- **Link to existing** links the group to a model you choose. Groups that include assets aren't offered consumable models.

If records in the group changed after the list loaded, the link is refused and nothing is linked. Refresh the page and review the group again. Every link is recorded in the audit log with the records it changed.

The suggested tracking type is only a starting point: groups that include assets are suggested as tracked assets, and other stock as consumables.

### Billing products

A model can name the billing product that sells it, so asset and billing records describe the same product. Prices and tax stay in billing. Choosing or changing the billing product needs billing access; people without it see only that a product is linked.

### Retire a model

**Retire** a model you no longer use. Records already linked keep their link and show the model as retired, but no new asset or stock item can be linked to it, and no new units of it can be received until you reactivate the model. Models are retired rather than deleted.

## Find an asset

1. Open **Assets**.
2. Enter a search term, select a category, or filter by state, such as **In repair**.
3. Choose the available sort order and use **Previous** and **Next** to move through results.
4. Open the asset to inspect its details, source, assignment and related service information.

Search and category selection apply across the accessible register, rather than only the rows currently shown. The matching count can change while others update records.

The register never shows an empty list in place of an error. If you don't have access, it says so. If loading fails, it says so and offers **Retry**. If a refresh fails after the list has loaded, the rows stay on screen with a notice giving the time they were loaded.

### Export the register

**Export** downloads every asset that matches your current search and filters as a CSV file, not only the page on screen. It includes the state, your organisation's status name, the purchase cost with its currency (or *Unknown*), and the source. It contains only assets you can see. Exports stop at 10,000 rows; the last line of the file says so, and you can narrow the filters to export the rest. Every export is recorded in the audit log.

### Select assets and print labels

Tick assets in the register to select them, across pages, up to 200 at a time. Changing the search or filters clears the selection. With assets selected you can:

- **Print labels**: one 4 × 2 inch label per asset, with a barcode or a QR code of the asset tag. Allow pop-ups for Enduria if your browser blocks the print window.
- **Export selected**: the same CSV as **Export**, for the selected assets only.
- **Open custody**: when one asset is selected.

On a phone, the register shows each asset as a card, and **Sections** opens the other asset pages.

## Create an asset

You need asset-create permission.

1. Select **New asset** from **Assets**.
2. Choose **Manual entry** for a record you will maintain yourself. Units already in stock are assets: open one and issue it from its **Stock** card instead of creating a new record.
3. Enter the **Asset name** and select a configured **Category**.
4. Add the serial number. For manual entry, optionally choose a **Catalogue model**; its category is used if you haven't chosen one.
5. Choose **Assigned to**, **Location**, and, for MSP records, **Client** as appropriate. The assigned person must be compatible with the asset's client.
6. Select a **Lifecycle stage** and enter purchase and warranty details when known.
7. Check the purchase currency against the purchase evidence, then select **Create & open** or **Create another**.

Open the saved record and confirm its tag, client and assignment. Stock quantities and connector observations have their own source rules; creating a register record does not prove physical receipt or a device's current condition.

A half-finished form is kept in your browser as a draft, so you can return to it. A draft belongs to your account in this organisation only. It is removed when you sign out or someone else signs in on the same browser, and after seven days. Purchase cost and notes are never kept in a draft.

## Update an asset

You need asset-edit permission. Open the asset and select **Edit**. Only the fields you change are saved.

- **Who holds the asset is not on this form.** To check an asset out, in or to someone else, use the asset's **Custody** tab (see [Track asset custody](./asset-custody)). For an asset in storage, the location shows its stockroom; move it with a custody check-in.
- **Facts the device reports.** In the current release, when the agent or a connector reports the manufacturer, model or serial number, the form shows it but doesn't change it. With [data quality](./asset-data-quality) (awaiting rollout), you can edit them and your value is kept: no connector or agent overwrites it, and a differing report appears in the inbox as a conflict.
- **If someone else changed the same field while you were editing,** nothing is saved. The form names the fields they changed. Select **Load their changes** to take their values for those fields; your other changes stay. Then check and save again.

Clear an optional field only when you intend to remove its value. Clearing purchase cost does not automatically clear purchase currency. A status can be any status of the asset's current state; custody and the [archive, disposal or restoration process](./asset-lifecycle) change the state.

## Read an asset's history

The asset's **History** tab lists, newest first:

- edits to its fields, with who made them;
- custody changes, repair case steps and warranty or support records;
- completed maintenance and audit sightings;
- archive, restoration, disposal and hold events;
- stock movements, for people with inventory access.

Use **Show** to list one kind of entry.

Edits record each field's old and new value, except purchase cost, notes and restricted [asset fields](./asset-fields#restricted-fields), which are recorded only as *changed*. People who can't see restricted fields see only that *a field changed*. Edits made by an API key, an import or a connector name that source; an edit whose source wasn't recorded says *Not recorded*.

History starts when your organisation began recording it. The tab says from which date, and earlier changes are not reconstructed. Agent and connector refreshes are not history entries; the asset shows when its source last reported instead.

**Tickets on this asset** lists the tickets that reference the asset and that you can open, newest activity first, with your organisation's status names. **View all** opens the ticket queue filtered to this asset.

Related tickets provide service context. They do not substitute for disposal evidence, approval records or proof of physical custody.

### Projects, configuration item and support dates

When available, the asset's **Overview** also shows these cards. They require a future application release.

**Projects** lists the projects that link this asset and that you can open, with each link's relationship and note. Projects you can't see are counted, not named. Link assets from a project's **Assets** section (see [Manage projects](./projects#link-assets)).

**Configuration item** shows whether the asset is recorded in **Services & CIs**, and if so:

- its class and owner;
- where it came from: recorded by a person, or adopted from a device provider, with when the provider last saw it;
- how many items it depends on, how many of those dependencies are not yet verified, and how many items depend on it.

**Open in Services & CIs** opens it there, where you can add and verify dependencies.

If the asset isn't recorded yet, people with `assets.manage` can select **Record as configuration item…**, give it a name and an optional class and description, and record it. It starts with no dependencies. A device seen by a provider is never recorded or verified automatically.

**Support and end of life** shows the support and end-of-life dates recorded for the asset's model and its reported operating system, or **Unknown** (see [Plan asset replacement](./asset-planning)).

## Record costs and read reports

Purchase cost and purchase currency are separate facts. Confirm currency from the original purchase evidence; do not assume an older asset used today's organisation preference. A known zero cost differs from an unknown cost.

Asset reporting keeps different currencies separate. Mixed or unknown currencies can leave a combined monetary figure unavailable; inspect the per-currency totals and unknown-currency counts. An unavailable total does not mean zero. Enduria does not convert currencies automatically, and these reports are not a substitute for accounting records.

Asset analytics follows your client access. An export additionally needs report-export authority. **Analytics → Assets** describes the current estate, excluding archived assets; it has no date range and does not reconstruct an earlier estate. Each figure's definition is shown when you point at it:

- **In use** counts assets in the *In use* state.
- **Maintenance overdue** counts assets with open maintenance work past its due date.
- **Warranty ending** counts recorded warranties ending in the next 90 days, today included.
- **Assignment rate** counts assets held by a person (staff or a customer contact) against all assets. It describes custody, not measured device use.

**Purchase cost** shows a total per currency and never converts. **By state** and **By status** use your organisation's status names.

## Understand device sources

Enduria uses supported vendor connectors for device management; it does not act as an MDM server. Review [integration setup](../admin/integrations) before enabling a provider. A recorded observation or last-seen time does not prove that a device is currently online or compliant.

An asset reported by the Enduria agent or a connector shows **Last reported** with the time and source. A manually recorded asset has no report time. On the asset's tabs, every reported fact says *Not reported* when the source didn't send it. It is never shown as zero.

- **Performance** (agent only) shows the newest sample from the last hour: CPU, memory, the fullest disk and network throughput, with a trend line. Samples older than 15 minutes are marked stale.
- **Hardware** shows what the device reported next to what was recorded on the asset.
- **Security** lists antivirus, firewall, disk encryption and pending updates. Each is *Passed*, *Failed*, *Unknown* (not reported) or *Stale* (reported more than seven days ago). These are the device's own observations, not a compliance assessment, and there is no score.
- **Software** lists the programs a device reported. An installed program is not evidence of a licence; licences are kept in **Licensing**.

## Show a location map

Asset pages can show a map of your configured locations, with the asset's location marked. The map is off until an administrator turns it on, in **Settings → Asset settings → Location map**, which needs settings-manage permission.

The map uses Google Maps: each viewer's browser loads map tiles from Google, and the names and coordinates of your locations are sent to Google to draw them. The setting is available only when your installation has a maps key. When the map is off, asset pages show the location's name instead.

Agent enrollment administration is restricted to authorised organisation-wide staff. Enrollment secrets are shown when issued and cannot be recovered from an ordinary list later. Retain them securely at issuance or generate a new enrollment as needed. Archiving revokes the asset's existing agent authority; restoration requires fresh enrollment.

## Resolve common problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A record or action is missing | Confirm the organisation, client assignment and current permissions. Check the archive if it is no longer active. |
| **You don't have access to…** | Your role lacks the permission for that data, for example ticket access for **Tickets on this asset**. Ask an administrator. |
| **Showing data from…** above the list | A refresh failed. The rows are from the time shown; select **Retry**. |
| **Someone else changed this asset while you were editing** | Select **Load their changes**, check the named fields, then save again. Nothing was saved. |
| Labels don't print | Allow pop-ups for Enduria in your browser, then print again. |
| An export ends with a note about 10,000 rows | Narrow the search or filters and export the remaining assets separately. |
| An assignee, location or category is rejected | Select a current record from your organisation and check the asset's client. |
| A catalogue model isn't offered for an asset | Consumable and retired models aren't offered for assets. Check the model's tracking type in **Settings → Models**. |
| **These records changed since you reviewed them** | Refresh **Unlinked records** and review the group again. Nothing was linked. |
| A tag conflicts | Another record may already use it. Choose a unique tag or use automatic allocation. |
| A report has no combined cost | Review unknown currencies, mixed currencies and missing purchase costs. |
| Imported device details change again | Check which fields the connector maintains. With [data quality](./asset-data-quality), a serial number, manufacturer or model you typed is kept. |
| A connected device didn't create an asset | It may match an asset you already have. Check **Assets → Data quality** (awaiting rollout). |
| A control described in a guide is absent | Check the guide's availability note and your service version with your administrator. |
