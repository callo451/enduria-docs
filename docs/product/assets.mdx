# Manage assets

Use **Assets** to keep a register of devices, their assigned people and clients, locations, purchase details, and related service work. You can add records manually, import a CSV, or use a supported device-management connector.

## Before you start

Your account needs asset-view permission to browse the register. Creating, editing, importing, archiving and reviewing require additional permissions. In an MSP organisation, staff assigned to a client remain limited to that client even when their role includes broad asset permissions. Customer portal access is separate from the staff workspace.

An administrator's title does not bypass these limits. See [users and permissions](../admin/users-and-permissions).

## Configure your register

Before adding a large number of assets, agree your organisation's categories, locations, lifecycle stages and asset-tag format.

- Open **Settings → Asset settings** to configure lifecycle values and tag formatting.
- Use **Categories** and **Locations** in the asset-management navigation for the corresponding reference records.
- Use your own configured lifecycle labels. Examples from another organisation may not be valid in yours.
- Keep parent categories and locations within your organisation. A hierarchy cannot contain cycles; resolve active child records before retiring a parent.

## Locations

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Shared locations, client sites and stockrooms require a future application release. Publishing documentation does not change your workspace.

</Admonition>

Assets and inventory use **one list of locations**, managed in **Settings → Asset locations**. A location can also be:

- **A client site.** Choose a client to make the location available only to that client's assets. Locations without a client are available to the whole organisation. Staff assigned to a client see organisation-wide locations and their own client's sites only.
- **A stockroom.** Select **Holds stock** for places that keep inventory stock or assets checked in to storage. Inventory items can only be kept in organisation-wide stockrooms; a client's stockroom can hold that client's stored assets.

When the asset and inventory location lists were combined, every inventory location was added to the shared list unchanged. If its code was already in use, the code gained a `-STOCK-` suffix. Locations that may duplicate another are marked **Review** with an explanation. Check them, then edit or retire the duplicate.

Locations are retired, not deleted, so history that names them stays readable. You can't retire a location, stop it holding stock, or restrict it to one client while it still holds stock or assets that don't fit.

Tag previews are examples, not reservations. The saved asset receives its allocated tag. Do not depend on consecutive numbers without gaps.

## Models

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

The model catalogue requires a future application release. Publishing documentation does not change your workspace.

</Admonition>

A **model** describes one product once, for example *Dell Latitude 5440*, so assets and inventory items can refer to the same thing. Manage models in **Settings → Models**. Each model has a manufacturer, a model name, an optional part number and asset category, and a **tracking** type:

- **Tracked asset**: each unit is its own asset record, such as a laptop or phone. In inventory, a tracked model's stock is its units, each received and issued by serial number (see [Manage inventory stock](./inventory#two-kinds-of-stock)).
- **Component**: a part that can be held as an asset record when it is worth tracking, such as a drive.
- **Consumable**: counted by quantity and used up, such as cables or toner. A consumable is never an asset record, so assets can't use consumable models.

Manufacturer and model names are matched ignoring case and extra spaces, so you can't create *Dell Latitude 5440* twice under different spellings.

When you add a model and choose its asset category, its tracking starts from the category's **Track new models by serial number** setting in **Settings → Asset categories**. You can still choose any tracking type. A model's tracking can't change while stock of it is held.

### Link a model to assets and stock

Choose a **Catalogue model** when you create or edit an asset or an inventory item. The record keeps the manufacturer and model text it was recorded or discovered with; choosing a model only fills those fields when they are blank. Units received into stock take the stock item's model.

### Link existing records

Existing assets and stock items are never linked automatically. **Settings → Models → Unlinked records** groups active assets and stock items that have no model by the manufacturer and model they were recorded with, ignoring case and spacing, and shows the other spellings found. For each group:

- **Create model** opens a new model filled in from the group. Saving it also links the group.
- **Link to existing** links the group to a model you choose. Groups that include assets aren't offered consumable models.

If records in the group changed after the list loaded, the link is refused and nothing is linked. Refresh the page and review the group again. Every link is recorded in the audit log with the records it changed.

The suggested tracking type is only a starting point: groups that include assets are suggested as tracked assets, and other stock as consumables.

### Billing products

A model can name the billing product that sells it, so asset and billing records describe the same product. Prices and tax stay in billing. Choosing or changing the billing product needs billing access; people without it see only that a product is linked.

### Retire a model

**Retire** a model you no longer use. Records already linked keep their link and show the model as retired, but no new asset or stock item can be linked to it, and no new units of it can be received until you reactivate the model. Models are retired rather than deleted.

## Find an asset

1. Open **Assets**.
2. Enter a search term or select a category.
3. Choose the available sort order and use **Previous** and **Next** to move through results.
4. Open the asset to inspect its details, source, assignment and related service information.

Search and category selection apply across the accessible register, rather than only the rows currently shown. The matching count can change while others update records. If loading fails, use **Retry** before relying on the results.

**Export page** downloads only the displayed page. It is not a complete estate export. The file includes the recorded purchase currency; unknown currency remains explicit.

## Create an asset

You need asset-create permission.

1. Select **New asset** from **Assets**.
2. Choose **Manual entry** for a record you will maintain yourself. Units already in stock are assets: open one and issue it from its **Stock** card instead of creating a new record.
3. Enter the **Asset name** and select a configured **Category**.
4. Add the serial number. For manual entry, optionally choose a **Catalogue model**; its category is used if you haven't chosen one.
5. Choose **Assigned to**, **Location**, and, for MSP records, **Client** as appropriate. The assigned person must be compatible with the asset's client.
6. Select a **Lifecycle stage** and enter purchase and warranty details when known.
7. Check the purchase currency against the purchase evidence, then select **Create & open** or **Create another**.

Open the saved record and confirm its tag, client and assignment. Stock quantities and connector observations have their own source rules; creating a register record does not prove physical receipt or a device's current condition.

## Update an asset

Open the record and use its edit action. Check the source before changing imported values: a later connector sync may refresh fields owned by the provider. Changing a client or assignee must still satisfy your current client access and the assignee's eligibility.

Clear an optional field only when you intend to remove its value. Clearing purchase cost does not automatically clear purchase currency. A lifecycle label by itself does not perform the [archive, disposal or restoration process](./asset-lifecycle).

Related tickets provide service context. They do not substitute for disposal evidence, approval records or proof of physical custody.

## Record costs and read reports

Purchase cost and purchase currency are separate facts. Confirm currency from the original purchase evidence; do not assume an older asset used today's organisation preference. A known zero cost differs from an unknown cost.

Asset reporting keeps different currencies separate. Mixed or unknown currencies can leave a combined monetary figure unavailable; inspect the per-currency totals and unknown-currency counts. An unavailable total does not mean zero. Enduria does not convert currencies automatically, and these reports are not a substitute for accounting records.

Asset analytics follows your client access. An export additionally needs report-export authority. A report's date range does not reconstruct a historical estate or change an asset's original purchase amount. Assignment rate describes recorded assignments, not measured device utilisation.

## Understand device sources

Enduria uses supported vendor connectors for device management; it does not act as an MDM server. Review [integration setup](../admin/integrations) before enabling a provider. A recorded observation or last-seen time does not prove that a device is currently online or compliant.

Agent enrollment administration is restricted to authorised organisation-wide staff. Enrollment secrets are shown when issued and cannot be recovered from an ordinary list later. Retain them securely at issuance or generate a new enrollment as needed. Archiving revokes the asset's existing agent authority; restoration requires fresh enrollment.

## Resolve common problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| A record or action is missing | Confirm the organisation, client assignment and current permissions. Check the archive if it is no longer active. |
| An assignee, location or category is rejected | Select a current record from your organisation and check the asset's client. |
| A catalogue model isn't offered for an asset | Consumable and retired models aren't offered for assets. Check the model's tracking type in **Settings → Models**. |
| **These records changed since you reviewed them** | Refresh **Unlinked records** and review the group again. Nothing was linked. |
| A tag conflicts | Another record may already use it. Choose a unique tag or use automatic allocation. |
| A report has no combined cost | Review unknown currencies, mixed currencies and missing purchase costs. |
| Imported device details change again | Check which fields the connector maintains. |
| A control described in a guide is absent | Check the guide's availability note and your service version with your administrator. |
