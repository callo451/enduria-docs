# Manage assets

The asset workspace combines manually maintained inventory with devices imported through configured management connectors.

## Find an asset

Search by name, asset tag, serial number, user, or other available fields. Filter by the categories, locations, and lifecycle states configured for your organisation.

## Keep records useful

- Assign the correct customer, owner, and location.
- Use the organisation's lifecycle states instead of free-form alternatives.
- Link related tickets to preserve service history.
- Confirm the source before editing connector-managed values; a later synchronisation may update provider-owned fields.

Enduria does not act as an MDM server. Device data comes from supported vendor connectors and is normalised into the asset inventory.
