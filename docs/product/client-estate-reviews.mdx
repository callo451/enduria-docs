# Client estate reviews

An estate review is a PDF summary of one client's estate that you prepare, check and publish to that client's portal. It answers the questions a client's IT contact asks in a quarterly review:

- what they have;
- who holds it;
- which devices have gone quiet;
- what warranty and support is ending;
- what coverage is in force;
- what is due for replacement, and roughly what it costs.

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Estate reviews require a future application release. Publishing this guidance does not deploy these features.

</Admonition>

Estate reviews are available to MSP organisations.

## What a review contains

Everything in a review is about the one client's active assets, as they stood when the review was generated:

| Section | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Your estate** | Active assets by category and by state |
| **Who holds what** | Assets held by the client's own people, by name. Counts of those held by your staff (never named), in storage, with a supplier, unassigned or lost. Loans past their due date. |
| **Devices not reporting** | Devices with an agent or connector that haven't reported within your organisation's threshold (see [Asset data quality](./asset-data-quality)), and counts of devices that report normally, never reported, or are recorded by hand |
| **Warranty and support** | Warranty, model support and operating system support ending within the period or already ended, and how many have no recorded date |
| **Coverage in force** | Warranty and support records in force, and whether each was verified |
| **Replacement outlook** | Assets due for replacement in the period, with why (see [Plan asset replacement](./asset-planning)). Expected replacement costs are totalled per currency and never converted; assets without an estimate are counted. |

A review never includes other clients, purchase prices, billing figures, internal notes, asset fields, network or hardware detail, or the names of your own staff.

## Prepare a review

You need asset view and manage permissions (`assets.view`, `assets.manage`). If your account is bound to a client, you can prepare reviews only for that client.

1. Open the client, select the **Assets** tab, and find **Estate reviews**.
2. Choose how far ahead to look (6, 12, 24 or 36 months) and select **Generate draft**.
3. In the draft:
   - Write a **Note for the client**, shown at the top of the review.
   - Untick replacement candidates you don't want to include; the estimate updates as you go.
   - Select **Preview PDF** to see exactly what will be published. A draft's PDF is marked as a draft.
   - Select **Save review** to keep your changes.
4. Select **Regenerate** if the estate has changed since you generated the draft. Your note is kept; unticked candidates are included again.

A draft is visible only to your staff. Drafts are deleted 30 days after they were last generated.

## Publish a review

Select **Publish**. The review gets the next number (EP-0001, EP-0002 and so on), its PDF is created and stored, and it appears in the client's portal. A published review never changes: to correct one, delete it and publish a new one.

Select **PDF** beside a published review to download it, for example to attach it to an email yourself. Enduria doesn't email reviews.

## What the client sees

The client's IT contacts, portal users with the **Client IT contact** role, see an **Estate reviews** section on the portal's **Devices** page once a review is published. They see their own client's published reviews, newest first, and can download each PDF. They never see drafts or other clients' reviews.

## Withdraw a review, and how long reviews are kept

**Delete** a published review to withdraw it from the portal; its record and PDF are removed. Published reviews are deleted automatically 24 months after publication.

Generating, publishing, regenerating, discarding, deleting and every download (by staff or in the portal) are recorded in the audit log.

## What estate reviews don't do

- **Delivery:** they aren't emailed, scheduled or generated automatically.
- **Customer actions:** clients can't comment on or acknowledge a review.
- **Agreements:** reviews don't link assets to agreements or support contracts.
- **Branding:** a review uses your organisation's name and brand colour, without per-client templates.
- **Accounting:** estimates are for planning, not quotes or accounting figures.
