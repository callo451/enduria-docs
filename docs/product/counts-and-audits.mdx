# Stock counts and asset audits

Counts and audits check your records against what is physically there. Both work the same way:

1. When you start one, Enduria **freezes what the records say** should be there.
2. People **record what they find**, by scanning labels or ticking items off.
3. Enduria lists the **differences**.
4. **A different person reviews them.** Nothing in stock or the asset register changes until they do.

<Admonition type="info" title="Awaiting rollout">

Stock counts and asset audits require a future application release. This guide describes the intended behaviour for that release; publishing documentation does not change your workspace.

</Admonition>

## Count a stockroom

1. Open **Inventory → Counts** and select **Start a count**.
2. Choose the stockroom. The count covers the stockroom and all its bins.
3. Leave **Blind count** on (recommended). Counters then don't see the quantities on record until the count is submitted, so they count what is there rather than confirming a number.
4. Select **Start count**.

On the count page:

- Type the quantity you count for each item and place.
- For items tracked by serial number, scan each unit's label (or type its tag or serial number and press Enter), or tick it off the list. A unit you scan that isn't expected here is recorded as seen.
- Select **Save counts** as you go, and **Submit count** when every line is counted.

**Stock can keep moving while you count.** Each line is compared with the stock at the moment you saved it, so an issue or receipt during the count doesn't show up as a difference.

If everything matches, the count completes straight away. Otherwise it waits for review.

### Review a count

A person with inventory management access, **who neither started nor submitted the count**, opens it and sees:

- each item that differs, and by how much;
- units that weren't found;
- units seen that weren't expected.

They either send the count back to be counted again, with a reason, or select **Approve and post corrections**. Approving:

- posts a correction for each difference, applied to the stock as it is now, so anything that moved since counting is kept;
- writes off each unit that wasn't found as lost, if it is still recorded in that stockroom;
- records what was done about each unexpected unit (a note is required for each).

Approval is the independent approval for these corrections, so they don't also need your stock approval settings. Each item's last count date is updated.

The quick **Count** action on an item still exists for one-off corrections, and still follows your adjustment approval settings.

## Audit assets at a place

1. Open **Assets → Audits** and select **Start an audit**.
2. Give it a title, then choose **Places** (each place includes everything beneath it) or **A client's sites**.
3. Select **Start audit**. The active assets recorded at those places are listed. Archived assets are left out.

On the audit page:

- Scan each asset's label (or type its tag or serial number) to record it as **seen here**, or use **Here** on its row.
- If an asset is somewhere else, select **Elsewhere** and choose where it was seen.
- If you scan an asset that isn't on the list, it is recorded as seen at the place you chose under **Where you are**.
- Select **Submit audit** when done. Anything not seen is listed as not found.

If every asset was seen where expected, the audit completes. Otherwise a person with asset management access, who neither started nor submitted it, decides each exception:

| Exception | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Not found | **Record as missing** (recorded as lost through custody, so your custody settings apply), or **Note it** with a reason |
| Found elsewhere, or not expected here | **Record the new location**, or **Note it** with a reason. An asset kept in storage at a stockroom must be moved through custody first. |

Completing the review applies these decisions. Every asset seen is stamped with the audit, and its page shows **Last audited** with a link to the audit.

## Permissions

| To | You need |
| --- | --- |
| Start, count and submit a stock count | Stock adjustment permission (inventory staff without a client restriction) |
| Review a stock count | Inventory management permission, and not having started or submitted it |
| Start, observe and submit an audit | Asset edit permission |
| Review an audit | Asset management permission, and not having started or submitted it |

Staff limited to one client can audit that client's sites only. Customers don't take part in audits.

## Resolve common problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| **Submit count** is disabled | Save your counts first, and count every line. |
| "Stock at this place has fallen below what the count would remove" | Stock moved out since the count. Send it back and count again. |
| A scan finds nothing | Scan the asset tag on the label, or type the tag or serial number. |
| **Record the new location** isn't offered | The asset is kept in storage at a stockroom. Move it through its Custody tab, then note the exception. |
| Recording an asset as missing is refused | Your custody settings require approval to record assets as lost. Ask for it on the asset's Custody tab first, then note the exception. |

## Current limits

- People can't confirm the assets they hold themselves; audits are carried out by staff.
- Scanning works with a USB or Bluetooth scanner that types into the page, or by typing. There is no camera scanning yet.
- Counts aren't scheduled automatically, and there are no value thresholds for reviewing differences.
