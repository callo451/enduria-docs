# Customer portal

The customer portal gives authorised customer users a branded place to request help, review their work, and read published knowledge.

The portal address and available blocks depend on your organisation's domain and branding configuration. A customer user only sees information available to their customer account and permissions.

## Typical tasks

- Submit a request through the service catalogue.
- Review status and conversation updates.
- Add replies or attachments where permitted.
- Search customer-visible knowledge.

Contact your service provider if you cannot access the portal or do not see an expected request.
