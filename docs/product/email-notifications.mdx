# Email from Enduria

## What you might receive

- **Account email** — invitations, password-reset links and welcome messages. These are always sent, whatever your preferences, because you need them to sign in.
- **Notifications** — updates about tickets, incidents, changes, projects and other work you are involved in, depending on how your organisation has set up notifications.
- **Digests** — a daily or weekly summary of your unread notifications, if you choose one.
- **Documents and reports** — invoices, quotes and scheduled reports sent to you.
- **Surveys** — a short satisfaction survey after a ticket is resolved, if your organisation uses them.

Emails show your organisation's name as the sender. Depending on your organisation's set-up, the sending address may belong to the operator of the service rather than your organisation; replies still go to your organisation.

## Reply to a ticket by email

If your organisation has connected a support mailbox, you can reply to a ticket notification by email. Keep the ticket number in the subject — for example `[TKT-00042]` — and your reply is added to the ticket.

## Choose which notification emails you get

If you have an account, open **Settings → Notifications**:

- Turn **Email Notifications** off to stop notification emails altogether. You still see notifications in Enduria.
- Under **Delivery Mode**, choose an email as each event happens, a **Daily Digest** or a **Weekly Digest**, and the time the digest arrives. With a digest, you get no separate email for each event.
- Turn individual kinds of notification off, for example when a ticket's status changes or someone comments.
- Set **Quiet Hours** to hold back notification emails during set times, read in your own time zone. Notifications during quiet hours are not emailed later; you still see them in Enduria.

These preferences apply to notification email only. Account email such as password resets is always sent.

## Unsubscribe

Notification emails, digests and scheduled reports have an **Unsubscribe** option in most mail apps, and an unsubscribe link. Using it:

- With an account, switches your notification email off. Turn it back on under **Settings → Notifications**.
- Without an account, stops that kind of email to your address — notifications, or that one scheduled report.

If the link opens a web page, the page asks you to confirm. Unsubscribing never stops password resets, invitations, invoices or quotes.

For survey eligibility, delivery timing and recovery, see [customer satisfaction surveys](../admin/customer-satisfaction).
