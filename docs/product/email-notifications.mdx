# Email from Enduria

## What you might receive

- **Account email** — invitations, password-reset links and welcome messages. These are always sent, whatever your notification preferences, because you need them to sign in.
- **Notifications** — updates about tickets, appointments and other work you are involved in, depending on how your organisation has set up notifications.
- **Documents and reports** — invoices, quotes and scheduled reports sent to you.
- **Surveys** — a short satisfaction survey after a ticket is resolved, if your organisation uses them.

Emails show your organisation's name as the sender. Depending on your organisation's set-up, the sending address may belong to the Enduria service rather than your organisation; replies still go to your organisation.

## Reply to a ticket by email

If your organisation has connected a support mailbox, you can reply to a ticket notification by email. Keep the ticket number in the subject — for example `[TKT-00042]` — and your reply is added to the ticket.

## Choose how often notifications arrive

Open **Settings → Notifications**:

- Turn **Email Notifications** off to stop notification emails.
- Under **Delivery Mode**, choose to receive each email as it happens, a daily summary, or a weekly summary, and the time the summary arrives.
- Pause notifications during specific times, such as outside working hours.

These preferences apply to notifications only. Account email such as password resets is always sent.
