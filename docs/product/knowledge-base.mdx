# Use the knowledge base

Search the knowledge base before resolving a repeated question or known issue. Articles can provide internal operating procedures or customer-facing guidance.

## Write an effective article

1. Use a task-oriented title that matches what a reader searches for.
2. State the outcome and prerequisites before the steps.
3. Keep each step focused on one action.
4. Add screenshots only when they clarify a control or state.
5. Put troubleshooting and rollback information near the relevant procedure.
6. Select a category configured for your organisation.

Use the review workflow before publication when required. Version history preserves earlier revisions, and published material can be exported when needed.
