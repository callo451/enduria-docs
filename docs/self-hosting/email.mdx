# Email

Each organisation configures its own outbound and inbound email in the application; see [How email works](../admin/email/overview). As the operator you decide whether to offer **platform email** — a sender you manage that organisations can use without their own SMTP server — and you provide the key that protects stored mail credentials.

Platform email is optional. Without it, organisations use custom SMTP, and the platform option is shown as unavailable.

## Platform email with Amazon SES

Platform email currently uses Amazon SES. Set all of the following; platform email is offered only when the access key, secret and From address are all present.

| Variable | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `AWS_SES_ACCESS_KEY_ID` | Access key for an IAM identity allowed to send through SES. |
| `AWS_SES_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` | Secret for that key. |
| `AWS_SES_REGION` | SES region. Defaults to `us-east-1`. |
| `AWS_SES_FROM_EMAIL` | The address all platform email is sent from, for example `noreply@notify.example.com`. Organisations cannot change it. |
| `AWS_SES_FROM_NAME` | Display name used only when an organisation has not set its own sender name. Defaults to `Enduria`. |
| `EMAIL_PLATFORM_MAX_PER_HOUR` | Most platform emails one organisation may send per hour. Defaults to `100`. |
| `EMAIL_PLATFORM_MAX_PER_DAY` | Most platform emails one organisation may send per day. Defaults to `1000`. |

Organisations may choose limits lower than yours, never higher. Their sender name appears as the display name and their Reply-To address receives replies; the From address is always yours.

### Prepare SES

1. **Use a sending subdomain** such as `notify.example.com`, so that mail sent on organisations' behalf cannot affect the reputation of your main domain.
2. **Verify the domain** in SES with Easy DKIM and publish the DKIM records it gives you.
3. **Configure a custom MAIL FROM domain** (for example `bounce.notify.example.com`) so SPF aligns with your From domain.
4. **Publish DMARC** for your domain, starting with `p=none` and a reporting address, then tightening once reports are clean.
5. **Grant least privilege.** The IAM identity needs `ses:SendEmail`, `ses:SendRawEmail` and `ses:GetIdentityVerificationAttributes` for your sending identity, and nothing else.
6. **Leave the SES sandbox** only when you are ready to send to unverified recipients. AWS reviews how you handle bounces and complaints.

:::caution
Enduria does not yet process SES bounce and complaint notifications or keep a suppression list. Until it does, monitor bounce and complaint rates in the SES console, and consider offering platform email only to organisations you trust. SES may pause an account whose bounce or complaint rate is too high, which stops platform email — including invitations — for every organisation that uses it.
:::

## Stored mail credentials

`EMAIL_ENCRYPTION_SECRET` encrypts the SMTP and IMAP passwords organisations save. Keep it stable and back it up with your other secrets: if it changes, stored mail passwords can no longer be decrypted and every organisation must re-enter them.

## Inbound mailbox checks

The scheduler checks each organisation's inbound mailboxes once a minute, honouring each mailbox's own polling interval. Inbound processing needs the scheduler service running with a valid `CRON_SECRET`; see [Configuration](./configuration).

## Check the configuration

After changing email variables, restart the application. Then, in an organisation:

1. Open **Settings → Email Integration** and confirm **Platform email** is available and shows your From address.
2. Select **Test Connection**.
3. Trigger a notification and confirm it arrives with the organisation's name and your From address.
